Cake Shop Game overview

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Cake Shop does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Developer

Developer account: Jinxin Wu

Information stored on your device

Cake Shop stores only the information needed to preserve your experience:

  • sound and music preferences;
  • tutorial completion status; and
  • local leaderboard records and game results.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to a remote server.

Accounts, analytics, advertising, and tracking

Cake Shop does not require an account and does not use cloud profiles, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. The app does not access your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, or camera.

Data sharing

Because the app does not collect personal data, the developer does not sell or share personal data with third parties. Apple may process information related to App Store downloads, purchases, diagnostics, or device services under Apple's own terms and privacy policy; that processing is outside Cake Shop.

Data retention and deletion

Cake Shop has no developer-operated server and therefore retains no personal data remotely. You can remove the information stored locally by deleting the app from your device.

Children's privacy

Cake Shop does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Jinxin Wu through the support link provided on the Cake Shop App Store product page.
